    How it works

    1. Muse decidesMuse Spark picks the apps, the risk (read, write or send) and the device to answer on.
    2. Wonder actsMuse Wonder calls the tools across Calendar, Slack, Gmail, MCP and the web.
    3. Tier gateCore enforces it, not the model: reads run, writes get a receipt, sends wait.
    4. A tap on your deviceThe send waits on your watch, and follows you to your phone if the watch comes off.
    5. ReceiptEvery change lands on your timeline with Undo, and Auren learns your habits from it.
